# Colorado Springs Trail Condition & Infrastructure Survey

A field-based GIS project documenting trail conditions and infrastructure in Colorado Springs, Colorado, using ArcGIS Survey123.

## Overview

Georeferenced field observations are collected with ArcGIS Survey123 while hiking local trails. Each observation records the observation type, condition, optional severity and maintenance notes, trail name, date and time, and optional photos. The dataset is being expanded as additional trails are surveyed.

## Observation Categories

- Trail surface
- Trail markers
- Signage and wayfinding
- Erosion
- Drainage
- Accessibility
- Obstructions
- Trail infrastructure
- Trash and litter

## Key Results

- 26 georeferenced field observations collected along Ute Trail.
- Trail markers (8) and trail surface (6) were the most frequently recorded observation types.
- 25 of 26 features were rated in Good condition. One erosion feature was rated Fair, and no Poor or Critical conditions were recorded.

## Workflow

Survey123 → ArcGIS Pro → QA/QC → ArcGIS Online → StoryMap

Observations are reviewed in ArcGIS Pro for completeness, consistency, and test records before being published as a hosted feature layer in ArcGIS Online.

## Limitations

Current observations were collected by a single observer in one session, and condition ratings are based on visual assessment. The dataset is not a comprehensive assessment of Colorado Springs trails.
